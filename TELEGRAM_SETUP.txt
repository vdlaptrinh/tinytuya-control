TinyTuya + Telegram Bot Setup
================================

1. TAO BOT TELEGRAM:
   - Mo Telegram, tim @BotFather
   - Gui /newbot
   - Nhap ten bot (vi du: TinyTuyaBot)
   - Nhan duoc BOT_TOKEN (string dai)

2. LAY BOT TOKEN:
   - Copy token nhan duoc tu BotFather
   - Vi du: 123456789:ABCdefGHIjklMNOpqrsTUVwxyz

3. KHOI DONG BOT:
   Cach 1: Truc tiep
   /opt/homebrew/bin/python3.11 /Users/dailuu/Desktop/CT2026/tuya2/telegram_bot.py <BOT_TOKEN>

   Cach 2: Dung script
   /Users/dailuu/Desktop/CT2026/tuya2/run_bot.sh <BOT_TOKEN>

4. LENH TELEGRAM:
   /start - Hien thi tro giup
   /list - Liet ke thiet bi
   /on <ten> - Bat thiet bi
   /off <ten> - Tat thiet bi
   /status <ten> - Xem trang thai
   /allon - Bat tat ca
   /alloff - Tat tat ca

   Vi du:
   /on Den_Ngu
   /off Den_Phong_Khach
   /status Den_Bep

5. TEN THIET BI (lay tu config.json):
   - Den_Ngu
   - Den_Nha_Ve_Sinh
   - Den_Gac
   - Den_Bep
   - Den_Phong_Khach
   - Den_Phong_Ngu
   - O_Cam_Ban_Hoc
   - IR_Thong_Minh

LUU Y:
- Chu y ten thiet bi phan biet hoa thuong
- Bot phai chay lien tuc (nen chay background)
- De chay nen: nohup /opt/homebrew/bin/python3.11 telegram_bot.py TOKEN &
